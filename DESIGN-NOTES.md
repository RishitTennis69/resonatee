# Resonate Studios redesign direction

## Audience and purpose

This landing page is for Bay Area parents evaluating an eight-week after-school program. It must make the program tangible, trustworthy, and easy to enroll in without asking visitors to decode startup language.

## One memorable idea

Students do not only learn about AI: they build, pitch, and defend a working product in front of real professionals.

## Named aesthetic

**California editorial workshop** — warm off-white paper, graphite typography, restrained safety-orange accents, precise grid lines, playful editorial moments, and believable program artifacts.

## Embarrassing failure modes

- Generic neon “AI startup” visuals or fake technical interfaces
- A daycare-like palette that undercuts the serious curriculum
- Repeated SaaS cards with no narrative progression
- Motion that competes with the copy or delays comprehension
- Invented testimonials, logos, or outcomes
- Desktop-only layouts that collapse carelessly on mobile

## Reference translation

- Mobbin: disciplined whitespace and compact navigation
- Databricks: direct orange accent and product-first visual hierarchy
- Wispr Flow: warm editorial surface, expressive serif moments, and crafted pacing

## Source-code visual audit

The supplied Resonate source establishes the actual production assets and behavior. The redesign therefore uses the original rocket logo, all five mentor portraits, and the real 1920×1080 Atlas and Revo capstone videos. Their original playback rates are preserved (2× for Atlas, 1.5× for Revo), with autoplay disabled when the visitor prefers reduced motion.
